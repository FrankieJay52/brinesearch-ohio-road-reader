Traffic Impacts in Trumbull County

Last modified: November 13, 2026

Local Conditions on OHGO

The following construction projects are anticipated to affect highways in Trumbull County next week. All scheduled work is weather permitting.

Interstate 80 in Hubbard Township
I-80 eastbound just west of the Pennsylvania State line has various lane restrictions for a pavement replacement project being overseen by the Pennsylvania Department of Transportation. Motorists should expect delays in this area.

U.S. Route 62 resurfacing, Brookfield & Hubbard Township
U.S. 62 from just south of I-80 to the Pennsylvania State line has various lane restrictions for pavement repairs and resurfacing. Estimated completion, late November.

U.S. Route 422 tree removal Southington Township
NEW IMPACT – On Monday, November 16, between 7 a.m. and 4 p.m., U.S. 422 between the Portage County line and SR 305 will be closed. The detour will be SR 305 to SR 282.

State Route 46 intersection improvements, Howland Township
CONTINUING IMPACT - The SR 46 and E. Market St. intersection has occasional lane restrictions with traffic maintained by flaggers for the addition of turn lanes through late November. Estimated completion, October 2027.

State Route 46 sidewalk installation, Weathersfield Township
SR 46 between Morris St. and Seaborn St. has various lane restrictions for sidewalk installation. Estimated completion, mid-December 2026.
