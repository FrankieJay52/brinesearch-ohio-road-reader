Traffic Impacts in Stark County

Last modified: November 13, 2026

Local Conditions on OHGO

The following construction projects are anticipated to affect highways in Stark County next week. All scheduled work is weather permitting.

State Route 212 railroad crossing repairs, Sugar Creek Township
SR 212 just west of SR 93 is NOW OPEN.

State Route 241 bridge replacement and roundabout construction, Jackson Township
SR 241 between Shuffel St. and Willowdale Lake Ave. is closed through fall 2027 for a bridge replacement and pavement reconstruction. The truck detour is SR 241 to I-77 to SR 687. The local detour is Shuffel St. to Frank Ave. to Strausser St.
