Traffic Impacts in Stark County

Last modified: October 23, 2026

Local Conditions on OHGO

The following construction projects are anticipated to affect highways in Stark County next week. All scheduled work is weather permitting.

U.S. Route 30 resurfacing, Canton, East Canton & Minerva
CONTINUING IMPACT - The following routes have various lane restrictions for roadway resurfacing and bridge repairs.
• U.S. 30 in Canton from Raff Rd. to SR 43.
• U.S.30 from Trump Avenue to the East Canton eastern corporation limit.
• U.S. 30 in Minerva from Liberty Avenue to the Columbiana County Line.
• SR 172 in East Canton from N Cedar Street to Browning Court.
Estimated completion, late October 2026.

U.S. Route 250 railroad crossing repairs, Sugar Creek Township
NEW IMPACT – Beginning Monday, November 2, U.S. 250 just north of the Tuscarawas County line will be closed through Thursday, November 5 for railroad crossing repairs. The detour will be SR 212 to SR 21.

State Routes 93, 172, 236 & 241 resurfacing, Massillon; Lawrence Township
SR 241 between Tremont Ave. and the Massillon Corporation limit and SR 172 between the Lincoln Way bridge over SR 21 and Erie St. has various lane restrictions for pavement repairs and resurfacing. Estimated completion, late October 2026.

State Route 212 railroad crossing repairs, Sugar Creek Township
NEW IMPACT – Beginning Tuesday, November 10, SR 212 just west of SR 93 will be closed through Friday, November 13. The detour will be U.S. 250 to SR 21.

State Route 241 bridge replacement and roundabout construction, Jackson Township
SR 241 between Shuffel St. and Willowdale Lake Ave. is closed through fall 2027 for a bridge replacement and pavement reconstruction. The truck detour is SR 241 to I-77 to SR 687. The local detour is Shuffel St. to Frank Ave. to Strausser St.
