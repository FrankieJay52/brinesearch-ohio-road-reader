Traffic Impacts in Harrison County

Last modified: October 23, 2026

Local Conditions on OHGO

The following ODOT construction projects are anticipated to impact traffic in Harrison County. All outlined work is weather permitting. Visit OHGO.com for travel updates or follow ODOT District 11 on Facebook or X for current Harrison County construction information.

U.S. Route 22 Projects

U.S. Route 22 major pavement rehabilitation: A project to remove and replace nearly 4 miles of pavement along the four-lane section of U.S. Route 22, from the two-lane section west of Cadiz to just west of County Road 13 (Upper Clearfork Road). Currently, U.S. 22 is reduced to one lane in both directions, with traffic maintained in the eastbound lanes. Motorists are advised to slow down when approaching the construction zone and watch for signage. Ramp closures will occur throughout construction. Estimated Completion: Summer 2028

Associated active work zones with U.S. 22 major pavement rehabilitation project:

U.S. 22 eastbound EXIT ramp closure for Cadiz (U.S. 250/SR 9): The U.S. 22 eastbound EXIT ramp for Cadiz/U.S. 250 east/SR 9 north is closed for 90 days. The closure is part of the ongoing U.S. 22 pavement rehabilitation project. The detour is to continue east on U.S. 22 to EXIT 224 (Jewett/Scio/SR 151 west), then take U.S. 22 west back to EXIT 220 (Cadiz/U.S. 250 east/SR 9 north).

North Main Street closure at U.S. 22: September 12-October 30, North Main Street is closed for two weeks as part of the ongoing U.S. 22 project. For access to North Main Street, traffic can detour via U.S. 22 to EXIT 220 for Cadiz, then take SR 9 south back to North Main Street. Estimated Reopening: October 30, 2026

State Route Projects

Currently, there are no active traffic impacts on State Routes in Harrison County.
