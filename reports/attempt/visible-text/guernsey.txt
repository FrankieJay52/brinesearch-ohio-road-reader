Traffic Impacts in Guernsey County

Last modified: October 26, 2026

Local Conditions on OHGO

The following ODOT construction projects are anticipated to impact traffic in Guernsey County. All outlined work is weather permitting. Visit OHGO.com for travel updates or follow ODOT District 5 on Facebook or Twitter for current Guernsey County construction information.

Interstate 70

Interstate 70 Pavement Repairs — Resurfacing and related roadway work along I-70 in Guernsey County. This project also includes replacing the concrete pavement at the I-70 and State Route 285 ramp approaches near Old Washington. Follow posted detours. Estimated project completion: Fall 2026.

Continued Impacts:

Motorist can expect lane closures in both directions at the I-77 interchange for striping operations.

Interstate 77

Interstate 77 Bridge Preservation – This project will repair the existing large diameter culvert under I-77 to extend its service life. Estimated project completion: December 2026.

New Impacts:

Old Twenty-One Road (CR 35) will close to traffic beginning Friday, Oct. 30.

U.S. and State Route Projects

U.S. Route 22 will close to traffic for stabilization work between Titus Road and Londonderry beginning Monday, Oct. 26. The roadway is expected to reopen Friday, Oct. 30.
