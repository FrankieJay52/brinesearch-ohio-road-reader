Traffic Impacts in Trumbull County

Last modified: October 23, 2026

Local Conditions on OHGO

The following construction projects are anticipated to affect highways in Trumbull County next week. All scheduled work is weather permitting.

Interstate 80 in Hubbard Township
I-80 eastbound just west of the Pennsylvania State line has various lane restrictions for a pavement replacement project being overseen by the Pennsylvania Department of Transportation. Motorists should expect delays in this area.

U.S. Route 62 resurfacing, Brookfield & Hubbard Township
U.S. 62 from just south of I-80 to the Pennsylvania State line has various lane restrictions for pavement repairs and resurfacing. Estimated completion, late October.

State Route 46 intersection improvements, Howland Township
NEW IMPACT – Beginning Tuesday, November 27, and continuing through Friday, October 30, traffic at the SR 46/E. Market St. intersection will be reduced to one lane with traffic maintained by flaggers daily between 6 a.m. and 7 p.m. for paving operations. Motorists can expect delays in the area.
CONTINUING IMPACT - The following restrictions are in place through mid-November 2026:
• Traffic on SR 46 southbound will not be able to turn onto East Market Street eastbound. The detour will be SR 46 south to SR 82 east to Howland Wilson Rd. to East Market St.
• Traffic on SR 46 northbound will not be able to turn onto East Market Street westbound. The detour will be SR 82 east to Howland Wilson Rd. to E. Market St.
• Traffic on East Market Street westbound will not be able to turn left onto SR 46 southbound. The detour will be Howland Wilson Rd. to SR 82 west to SR 46.
CONTINUING IMPACT - The SR 46 and E. Market St. intersection has various lane restrictions with traffic maintained by flaggers for the addition of turn lanes. Estimated completion, October 2027.

State Route 46 sidewalk installation, Weathersfield Township
NEW IMPACT – SR 46 between Morris St. and Seaborn St. has various lane restrictions for sidewalk installation. Estimated completion, mid-December 2026.
