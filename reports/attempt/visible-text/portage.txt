Traffic Impacts in Portage County

Last modified: October 23, 2026

Local Conditions on OHGO

The following construction projects are anticipated to affect highways in Portage County next week. All scheduled work is weather permitting.

Interstate 76 bridge repairs, Rootstown & Edinburg Townships
I-76 eastbound and westbound between SR 44 and Industry Rd. will have various lane restrictions nightly between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. through late October for resurfacing. Estimated completion, late October 2026.

U.S. Route 422 culvert replacement, Nelson Township
NEW IMPACT – U.S. 422 between Bancroft Rd. and Hobart Rd. is closed through Wednesday, October 28 for a culvert replacement. The detour is U.S. 422 to SR 282 to SR 305.

State Route 14 & State Route 43 intersection improvements, Streetsboro
CONTINUING IMPACT – The following routes have various nightly lane restrictions through late October for resurfacing:
• SR 14 from just south of I-80/Ohio Turnpike to Portage Pointe Dr.
• SR 43 from the southern Streetsboro Corporation limit to Market Square Dr.
CONTINUING IMPACT – SR 303 at SR 14 is closed through late October. The detour is Superior Ave. to SR 14.
Estimated completion, late November 2026.

State Route 225 culvert replacement, Palmyra Township
NEW IMPACT – SR 225 between Williams Rd. and Corbett Rd. is closed through Tuesday, October 27 for a culvert replacement. The detour is SR 225 to I-76 to SR 14.
