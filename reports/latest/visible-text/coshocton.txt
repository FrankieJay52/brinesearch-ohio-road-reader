Traffic Impacts in Coshocton County

Last modified: October 29, 2026

Local Conditions on OHGO

The following ODOT construction projects are anticipated to impact traffic in Coshocton County. All outlined work is weather permitting. Visit OHGO.com for travel updates or follow ODOT District 5 on Facebook or Twitter for current Coshocton County construction information.

U.S. and State Route Projects

State Route 93 will experience daytime lane closures between U.S. Route 36 and the Holmes County line for paving operations. Both directions of travel will be maintained by flaggers. Estimated project completion: Fall 2027.
