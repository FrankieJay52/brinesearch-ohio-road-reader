Traffic Impacts in Mahoning County

Last modified: October 23, 2026

Local Conditions on OHGO

The following construction projects are anticipated to affect highways in Mahoning County next week. All scheduled work is weather permitting.

Interstate 76/80 pavement rehabilitation, Austintown & Jackson Townships
CONTINUING IMPACT – I-80 east and west is reduced to two lanes between the Meander Reservoir and SR 46. Estimated completion, mid-November 2026.

Interstate 680 pavement replacement, Youngstown
CONTINUING IMPACT – I-680 southbound traffic is crossed over onto the northbound lanes through late fall while I-680 southbound is reconstructed.
CONTINUING IMPACT – I-680 northbound between South Ave. and SR 193 is CLOSED through summer 2027. The detour is U.S. 62 to U.S. 422 to SR 193.
CONTINUING IMPACT – The following ramps are closed through late fall 2026:
• Sillman St to I-680 southbound. The detour is Steel St. to Mahoning Ave. to Belle Vista Ave. to Oakwood St. to I-680.
• SR 193 to I-680 southbound. The detour is I-680 northbound to SR 711 to U.S. 422 eastbound to U.S.62 west/SR 7 south to I-680 south.
• I-680 SB to Glenwood Ave./Mahoning Ave. The detour is I-680 southbound to South Ave. to Front St. to Marshall St.
• Glenwood Ave./Mahoning Ave to I-680 south. The detour is High St. to Oak Hill Rd. to Falls Ave. to Market St. to Williams Ave. to South Ave.
• I-680 south to Market St. south. The detour is I-680 south to South Ave. to Williamson Ave. to Market St.
• I-680 south to Market St. north. The detour is I-680 south to South Ave. to Williamson Ave. to Market St.
• I-680 south to U.S. 62/SR 7 north. The detour is I-680 south to South Ave. to U.S.62/SR 7.
CONTINUING IMPACT – The following ramps are closed through summer 2027:
• I-680 north to SR 193. The detour is U.S. 62/SR 7 to U.S. 422 to SR 193.
• Marshall St. to I-680 north. The detour is Glenwood Ave. to Mahoning Ave. to Belle Vista Ave.
• I-680 north to Glenwood Ave./Mahoning Ave. The detour is South Ave. to Front St. to Marshall St.
• Market St. to I-680 north. The detour is Woodland Ave. to Hadnet Dr. to South Ave. to U.S. 62/SR 7 to U.S. 422 to SR 193.
• Woodland Ave. to I-680 north. The detour is Woodland Ave. to Hadnet Dr. to South Ave. to U.S. 62/SR 7 to U.S. 422 to SR 193.
• U.S. 62 west/SR 7 south to I- 680 north. The detour is Woodland Ave. to Hadnet Dr. to South Ave. to U.S. 62/SR 7 to U.S. 422 to SR 193.
Estimated completion, August 2028.
