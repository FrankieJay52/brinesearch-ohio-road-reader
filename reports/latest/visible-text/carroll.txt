Traffic Impacts in Carroll County

Last modified: October 23, 2026

Local Conditions on OHGO

The following ODOT construction projects are anticipated to impact traffic in Carroll County. All outlined work is weather permitting. Visit OHGO.com for travel updates or follow ODOT District 11 on Facebook or X for current Carroll County construction information.

State Route 164 chip sealing preventative maintenance: A project to chip seal SR 164 from Bergholz in Jefferson County through Carroll County, ending in Salineville. Traffic is maintained with flaggers. Estimated Completion: October 31, 2026.

Grant Street Boulevard closure in Minerva: Grant Boulevard is closed between South Market Street and Murray Avenue to replace the bridge over Sandy Creek. Estimated completion: October 31, 2026.
