Traffic Impacts in Tuscarawas County

Last modified: October 20, 2026

Local Conditions on OHGO

The following ODOT construction projects are anticipated to impact traffic in Belmont County. All outlined work is weather permitting. Visit OHGO.com for travel updates or follow ODOT District 11 on Facebook or X for current Tuscarawas County construction information.

Interstate 77 Projects

I-77 pavement repairs at Newcomerstown Exit 65: October 13-23, I-77 will be reduced to one lane at Exit 65 (Newcomerstown/U.S. 36) for pavement repair work.

State Route Projects

U.S. Route 250 closure near Beach City: November 2-5, U.S. 250 will close just west of SR 93 for railroad crossing repairs located at the Stark County line. The detour is SR 212 east to SR 21 south, and reverse.

State Route 258 closure near West Chester: November 9-20, SR 258 will close near West Chester for culvert replacement work. The detour is SR 800 north to U.S. 250 west, U.S. 250 west to U.S. 36 west, U.S. 36 west back to SR 258, and reverse. Estimated Completion: November 20, 2026.

Local Projects

West 3rd Street closure in Uhrichsville: The West 3rd Street bridge is closed from Water Street to West Jay Street for a bridge replacement project from July 13-October 31. Estimated Completion: October 31, 2026.

County Road 68 (Barnhill Road) closure: Effective immediately, the Barnhill Road bridge over U.S. Route 250 in Midvale is closed to all traffic due to structural damage. The ramps to/from U.S. Route 250 will remain open at the interchange, though drivers cannot cross the bridge. ODOT is actively developing a permanent repair solution. An exact reopening date is currently unknown. ODOT will provide further updates as project plans and timelines are finalized. Learn more about the closure.
