Traffic Impacts in Jefferson County

Last modified: October 30, 2026

Local Conditions on OHGO

The following ODOT construction projects are anticipated to impact traffic in Jefferson County. All outlined work is weather permitting. Visit OHGO.com for travel updates or follow ODOT District 11 on Facebook or X for current Jefferson County construction information.

State Route 7 Projects

State Route 7 southbound 4 The SR 7 southbound on-ramp from 4th Street in Toronto is closed to traffic as part of a mine subsidence restoration project. To access SR 7 southbound, traffic is rerouted northbound to the SR 152 exit, then returns to SR 7 southbound. Estimated Completion: November 14, 2026.

State Route 7 mine subsidence restoration: A mine subsidence restoration project on SR 7 just south of the 4th Street EXIT ramp in Toronto. One lane of traffic is maintained during construction. Estimated Completion: November 14, 2026

State Route 7 resurfacing: The project to resurface SR 7 from Brilliant to Steubenville is nearing completion. Remaining work includes pavement markings, raised pavement markers, and concrete repair work. One lane of traffic will be maintained in both directions during active work. Estimated Completion: November 30. 2026

State Route 7 Northbound On-Ramp closure at Logan Avenue: The Logan Avenue ramp to SR 7 northbound is closed indefinitely due to a recurring slope failure. Since January 2025, the slope has repeatedly failed, prompting the ramp to be closed for safety until repairs can be made. Construction will begin in the coming months as ODOT awaits required approvals from the adjacent railroad. Estimated Completion: November 30, 2027

Other State Routes

State Route 152 closure north of Richmond: November 9-20, SR 152 will close during daytime hours for culvert replacement work. Work hours are 7:30 a.m. to 3 p.m., Monday through Friday, except Veterans Day (November 11). The detour is SR 43 south to U.S. 22 east, U.S. 22 east to SR 213 north, SR 213 north back to SR 152, and reverse.

State Route 213 resurfacing: Resurface nearly nine miles of SR 213 from SR 7 in Steubenville to SR 152 in Knoxville. One lane of traffic is maintained with flaggers. Estimated Completion: November 30, 2026

State Route 164 chip sealing preventative maintenance: A project to chip seal SR 164 from Bergholz in Jefferson County through Carroll County, ending in Salineville. Traffic is maintained with flaggers. Estimated Completion: September 30, 2026. Learn more about ODOT’s chip sealing preventative maintenance treatment.

Local Projects

Reese Street bridge closure in Irondale: The Reese Street bridge, just south of Saline Street in Irondale, is closed for a bridge replacement. Estimated Completion: December 31, 2026

West Virginia DOT Traffic Impacts

Visit 511WV for traffic impacts in West Virginia. All information shared is provided by WVDOT.

U.S. Route 22 Veterans Memorial Bridge nighttime closures: A portion of US 22, eastbound and westbound, on the Veterans Memorial Bridge, will have both the eastbound and westbound lanes closed on the following anticipated closure dates:

November 23rd, 8:00 PM - 5:00 AM

December 2nd, 8:00 PM - 5:00 AM

Closure 5: December 9th, 8:00 PM - 5:00 AM

Closure 6: December 15th, 8:00 PM - 5:00 AM

Closure 7: December 22nd, 8:00 PM - 5:00 AM

The closures are necessary to replace cable. The closures will be at night to minimize the impact on the traveling public. Motorists should follow detour signs and use an alternate route. Emergency vehicles will be accommodated.

Alternate Routes: Northbound traffic should use US 30 to East Liverpool, Ohio/Chester, WV, via the Jennings-Randolph Bridge, to WV 2 and Ohio 7. Southbound traffic should use Ohio 7 and WV 2 to Brilliant, Ohio, and Wellsburg, WV, onto the Wellsburg Bridge.
