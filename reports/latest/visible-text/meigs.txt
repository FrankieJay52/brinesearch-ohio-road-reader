Traffic Impacts in Meigs County

Last modified: October 21, 2026

Local Conditions on OHGO

The following ODOT construction projects are anticipated to impact traffic in Meigs County. All outlined work is weather permitting. Visit OHGO.com for travel updates or follow ODOT District 10 on Facebook or X for current Meigs County construction information.

U.S. 33 Expansion (Athens and Meigs Counties)
A two-lane to four-lane expansion project is taking place on U.S. 33 from Athens to Darwin and Five Points to Ravenswood.

Barrels are in place on U.S. 33 between Five Points and the Ravenswood Bridge to delineate between the current road and road under construction (through 2028)

Barrier wall is in place through November 1, 2026, creating a new traffic pattern on U.S. 33 west between SR 7 and Pine Grove Road

Through 2028, there will be intermittent flagging at SR 681 for equipment crossing

Through 2028, there will be intermittent closures of side roads between Pleasanton Rd. (Athens County) and Rocksprings Rd. (Meigs County) for equipment crossing

Read more about the project

Overall Project Completion: 2028

U.S. 33-SR 7-SR 124-SR 733-SR 833 Sign Replacements
A sign replacement project is taking place on U.S. 33, SR 7, SR 124, SR 733, and SR 833 at Rock Springs and Five Points. There are intermittent lane and shoulder closures in each direction.
Estimated Completion: November 30

State Route 681 Slope Stabilization
A slope stabilization project begins on October 26 on SR 681 between Stevens Road and Harris Road. The road will be closed from 7:30 a.m.-4 p.m.
Estimated Completion: October 30

State Route 681 Slope Stabilization
A slope stabilization project is taking place on SR 681 between Goose Creek Road and Carsey Road. Traffic is being maintained by flaggers.
Estimated Completion: November 2027

State Route 325 Culvert Replacement
A culvert repair project is taking place on SR 325 between Edmundson Road and Sanford Davis Road. Traffic is being maintained with temporary signals.
Estimated Completion: January 1
