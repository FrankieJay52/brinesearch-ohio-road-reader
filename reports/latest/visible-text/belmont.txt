Traffic Impacts in Belmont County

Last modified: November 06, 2026

Local Conditions on OHGO

The following ODOT construction projects are anticipated to impact traffic in Belmont County. All outlined work is weather permitting. Visit OHGO.com for travel updates or follow ODOT District 11 on Facebook or X for current Belmont County construction information.

Interstate 70 Projects

I-70/State Route 149 interchange improvement: Work is underway on the major interchange improvement project at the I-70 Exit 208 for SR 149/Morristown. I-70 currently has two lanes open in both directions. Future traffic impacts for SR 149 will begin in 2027. For project details and all traffic impacts, visit the project webpage. Estimated Completion: 2029

U.S. Route and State Route Projects

Currently, there are no active U.S. or State Route projects in Belmont County.
