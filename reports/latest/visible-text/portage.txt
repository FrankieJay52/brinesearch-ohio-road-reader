Traffic Impacts in Portage County

Last modified: October 30, 2026

Local Conditions on OHGO

The following construction projects are anticipated to affect highways in Portage County next week. All scheduled work is weather permitting.

Interstate 76 bridge repairs, Rootstown & Edinburg Townships
I-76 eastbound and westbound between SR 44 and Industry Rd. will have various lane restrictions nightly between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. through early November for resurfacing. Estimated completion, early November 2026.

U.S. Route 422 culvert replacement, Nelson Township
NEW IMPACT – U.S. 422 between Bancroft Rd. and Hobart Rd. is NOW OPEN.

State Route 14 & State Route 43 intersection improvements, Streetsboro
CONTINUING IMPACT – SR 303 at SR 14 is closed through late November. The detour is Superior Ave. to SR 14.
Estimated completion, late November 2026.

State Route 225 culvert replacement, Palmyra Township
NEW IMPACT – SR 225 between Williams Rd. and Corbett Rd. is NOW OPEN.
