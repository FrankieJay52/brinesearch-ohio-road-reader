Traffic Impacts in Noble County

Last modified: October 21, 2026

Local Conditions on OHGO

The following ODOT construction projects are anticipated to impact traffic in Noble County. All outlined work is weather permitting. Visit OHGO.com for travel updates or follow ODOT District 10 on Facebook or X for current Noble County construction information.

State Route 821 Landslide
Due to a landslide, traffic is being maintained with temporary signals on SR 821 between Baker Junkyard Road and I-77.
Estimated Completion: December 1

State Route 78 Bridge Replacement
A bridge replacement project is taking place on SR 78 between Cain Road and Collins Road. Traffic is being maintained with temporary signals.
Estimated Completion: November 1
