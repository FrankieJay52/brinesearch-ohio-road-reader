Traffic Impacts in Muskingum County

Last modified: October 22, 2026

Local Conditions on OHGO

The following ODOT construction projects are anticipated to impact traffic in Muskingum County. All outlined work is weather permitting. Visit OHGO.com for travel updates or follow ODOT District 5 on Facebook or Twitter for current Muskingum County construction information.

Interstate 70

Reconstruction in Zanesville - This project will reconstruct I-70 though the city of Zanesville, resurface I-70 between U.S. 40 and State Route 93. Full reconstruction will begin west of the Licking Road overpass, including the Maple Avenue ramps and Maple Avenue at the end of the ramps, and end east of the abandoned railroad overpass. The project also impacts 16 bridges in the area. For more project details, visit the project page. Estimated Completion: Fall 2028.

New Impacts:

Sixth Street under I-70 is expected to reopen the week of Monday, Oct. 26.

The westbound right lane will close to traffic overnight Thursday, Oct. 29 for bridge work. It will reopen the next morning.

Licking Road will close to traffic overnight Thursday, Oct. 29 for bridge work. It will reopen the next morning.

Continued Impacts:

Motorist should anticipate lane closures on the Hamline Bridge maintained by temporary signals until Friday, Oct. 23.

Phase three configuration is now in place. If the contractor must take a lane at overnight, the westbound driving lane will close, and the only access to downtown from I-70 westbound will be U.S. 40 (exit 152) and SR 93 (exit 157).

I-70 westbound passing lane operates like an express lane. This means that once motorists enter the express lane after SR 93 (exit 157), they will be unable to exit until U.S. 40 (exit 152).

The I-70 westbound driving lane will be the only westbound lane with access to downtown Zanesville.

The Underwood Street eastbound on ramp is open to traffic.

The State Street eastbound on ramp is open to traffic.

The Sixth Street westbound on ramp is closed to traffic.

The 7th Street eastbound off ramp is open to traffic.

The Underwood off-ramp is open to traffic.

The Maple Avenue westbound off ramp is closed to traffic.

The Maple Avenue flyover ramp and the 6th Street on-ramp to I-70 westbound will remain closed during Phase three construction for safety reasons.

All work is weather dependent, and dates are subject to change.

U.S. and State Route Projects

State Route 555 is closed to traffic between Pletcher Hill Road and Bush Hill Road. Estimated project completion: November 2026. Detour: SR 555 to SR 669 to SR 93 to U.S. Route 22 to SR 719 to SR 555 and reverse.

Motorists traveling State Route 60 can expect lane closures for resurfacing between SR 555 and Duncan Falls. Estimated project completion: October 2026.
